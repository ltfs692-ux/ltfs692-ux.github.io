SAIF LLC-FZ — Final B2B Platform Demo Guide

This preview demonstrates the complete customer-to-ERP business workflow. Production deployment requires the live database and selected external API credentials.

Customer Journey

1. Explore & Build Inquiry
The customer browses the 12-product catalogue, sees public B2B prices in the selected market currency, adds multiple products and chooses quantities.
2. Login Only at Inquiry Submission
When Create Inquiry is selected, the customer logs in or creates a business account using Email, Mobile OTP preview, Google preview or registration.
3. Inquiry Appears in ERP
Sales sees the inquiry. Stock and reorder rules are checked immediately. Procurement alerts are created immediately from stock/reorder rules. Procurement waits only for Super Admin approval — it does not wait for customer quote acceptance.
4. Quote + Management Approval
Sales prepares the quotation. Super Admin/Management approves it. Only then is it delivered to the customer portal. Email and WhatsApp delivery use the same document once APIs are connected.
5. Customer Approves or Negotiates
The customer sees Quotation Received, not internal ERP wording. They can Approve, Negotiate target prices, or open a WhatsApp query with the quotation number.
6. Revised Quote
If negotiation is submitted, Sales creates a new version, Management approves it, and the revised quotation is delivered again.
7. Stock Check + Procurement
Procurement may already be running before final customer acceptance. Super Admin approves the RFQ, mapped suppliers are compared, and a Purchase Order can be created independently. When the customer later accepts the final quote, the Sales Order links to the existing procurement instead of creating duplicates.
8. Goods Receipt
Receiving a supplier PO increases physical stock and posts Inventory / Accounts Payable accounting automatically.
9. Payment
Country-specific options are shown. Card is instant in the preview. Cash/Bank/local methods wait for Finance approval. Approved customer credit consumes the credit limit and remains receivable until actual settlement.
10. Shipment & Receipt
When stock and payment/credit approval are ready, Super Admin dispatches the shipment. Inventory is deducted at dispatch. The customer confirms receipt in My Account.
11. Invoice, Finance & Audit
Invoices, actual payments, receivables, supplier payables, inventory value, COGS, journal entries, trial balance and audit trail update from the connected actions.

Finance Explanation

No invented opening values: V5.3 starts Bank/Cash and financial Inventory Asset at AED 0 until Finance/Super Admin uses Set Opening Balances. Product average cost is also 0 until configured or learned from a supplier goods receipt.
  • Invoice: Accounts Receivable DR = Sales Revenue CR + VAT Payable CR
  • Customer payment: Bank/Cash DR = Accounts Receivable CR
  • Supplier goods receipt: Inventory Asset DR = Accounts Payable CR
  • Supplier payment: Accounts Payable DR = Bank/Cash CR
  • Shipment: COGS DR = Inventory Asset CR
Credit control: Customer Credit is not actual cash. It reduces available credit, creates an outstanding balance and can be settled later. Once settlement is confirmed, available credit is restored.
