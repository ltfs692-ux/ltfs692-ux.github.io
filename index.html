About us

A flexible B2B trading platform

SAIF is presented as a multi-category trading business with a customer journey designed around enquiries, controlled quotations, negotiation, stock checks, procurement, payments and finance. The Second Demo focuses on the complete B2B journey while keeping the architecture ready for future expansion.

Customer journey

Simple outside. Connected inside.

Customers only see the steps relevant to them. Internal approvals and procurement stay in the ERP.

01

Explore

Browse catalogue, product details and visible public B2B prices.

02

Create inquiry

Add multiple products and quantities. Login is requested only at inquiry submission.

03

Receive quote

After internal approval, the quotation appears in the customer account.

04

Approve or negotiate

Accept the quote or submit requested prices and discuss the quote.

05

Pay & invoice

After final acceptance and stock approval, payment is enabled and the invoice is generated.
